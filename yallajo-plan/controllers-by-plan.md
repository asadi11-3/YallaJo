# YallaJo Controllers — Mapped to Plans

> Generated by code-first audit (2026-06-09). Source of truth: shipped controllers in
> `src/Hosts/YallaJo.Web/`, cross-referenced against the area plans in
> `yallajo-plan/` (`1-public-storefront.md` through `8-superadmin-rbac.md`).
>
> **Totals:** 128 controller files = 127 active MVC controllers + 1 infra base
> (`BaseController.cs`).
>
> **Dead/orphaned (commented out 2026-06-09):**
> - `Areas/Provider/Controllers/EarningsController.cs` — no inbound reference; Earnings UI lives on `/provider/finance` via `FinanceController`. Sibling view `Areas/Provider/Views/Earnings/Index.cshtml` left in place as documented orphan.
>
> **Redirect-only (kept — reachable, no view of their own):**
> - `Areas/Auth/Controllers/DevicesController.cs` — nav-link target, redirects to Sessions. The unused view `Areas/Auth/Views/Devices/Index.cshtml` is the orphan; the controller stays.
> - `Areas/Admin/Controllers/LifecycleController.cs` — POST-only lifecycle mutations, PRG-redirect to Users/Details.
> - `Areas/Public/Controllers/CultureController.cs` — POST language switcher (forms in `_Navbar.cshtml`).
> - `Features/Notifications/NotificationsController.cs` — mark-read/mark-all-read POST forms (bell + Overview + Notifications views).
